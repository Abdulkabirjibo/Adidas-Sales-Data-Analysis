# Adidas US Sales Analysis (2020–2021)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Adidas sales performance across the United States between 2020 and 2021 using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to evaluate sales trends, product performance, retailer contributions, regional performance, and profitability to identify business opportunities and support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Charts and Visualizations
- Excel Dashboard
- Data Analysis and Reporting

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains 9,644 sales records and includes information such as:

- Retailer and Retailer ID
- Invoice Date
- Region, State, and City
- Product Category
- Price per Unit
- Units Sold
- Total Sales
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin
- Sales Method (Online, Outlet, and In-store)

## Business Questions
The analysis focused on answering the following questions:

1. Which retailers generate the highest sales and profit?
2. Which Adidas product categories perform best?
3. Which US regions contribute the most to sales?
4. Which sales method generates the highest revenue?
5. What is the overall profitability of Adidas US sales?
6. How can sales performance be improved?

## Key Insights

### 1. Product Performance
- Men's Street Footwear was the highest-performing product category, generating approximately $27.68 million in calculated sales.
- Women's Apparel followed with approximately $23.87 million.
- Women's Athletic Footwear recorded the lowest sales among the six product categories.

### 2. Regional Performance
- The West region generated the highest calculated sales, approximately $36.44 million.
- The Midwest region recorded the lowest calculated sales, approximately $16.67 million.

### 3. Retailer Performance
- West Gear was the leading retailer by calculated operating profit, generating approximately $12.20 million.
- Foot Locker and Sports Direct were also major contributors to profitability.

### 4. Sales Method Performance
- Online sales generated approximately $44.97 million in calculated sales.
- Outlet sales generated approximately $39.54 million.
- In-store sales generated approximately $35.66 million.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Strengthen Men's Street Footwear:** Maintain stock availability and consider expanding marketing efforts for this high-performing category.
2. **Improve Midwest Performance:** Investigate the factors behind lower regional sales and develop targeted promotional strategies.
3. **Invest in Online Sales:** Continue improving the online shopping experience because it generated the highest sales among the three sales channels.
4. **Leverage Top Retailers:** Strengthen partnerships with high-performing retailers such as West Gear and Foot Locker.
5. **Improve Underperforming Categories:** Explore pricing, marketing, and product positioning opportunities for Women's Athletic Footwear.
